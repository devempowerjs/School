Aamir, ye poora matter sir ko batate time isi sequence mein batana. Jo hua hai wahi exactly batana, kuch extra add ya change mat karna.

Independence Day se kuch din pehle Salman wagera washroom mein paani fek-feki kar rahe the. Tum aur Nishant unko bolne gaye ki aisa mat karo. Uske baad lunch mein tumne ye baat hum logon ko batayi—Mayank, Uday, Sudhanshu aur Aditya ko. Un logon ne dhamki di ki lunch break mein aao. Hum log gaye aur unse baat ki. Mayank, Uday aur Sudhanshu ne baat karke matter wahi solve kar diya. Koi ladai ya jagda nahi hua, normal baat se matter khatam ho gaya.

Uske baad Independence Day ke din sab log ground mein the. Salman, Samar aur Saban ne tumhe akele mein roka. Dhakka-mukki aur gaali-galauj hui. Un logon ne tumhe dhakka maara, tumne bhi react kiya, phir un logon ne tumhe thappad maara. Tumhara chashma gir gaya aur gaal par 1-2 drops bleeding bhi hui. Tumne hum logon ko bataya, phir hum sab unki class mein gaye. Wahan mam ko poori baat batayi.
Mam ne Salman ko bulaya, lekin mam ke saamne bhi woh tez banne laga aur baat garam ho gayi. Phir Prince Sir aaye, dono ko staff room le gaye aur application wagaira likhwai. Hume laga ki matter yahin solve ho gaya.

Lekin uske baad Salman, Samar aur Saban baar-baar mauka dhoondhne lage ki tumhe akele mein milkar maar sakein, kyunki complaint ho gayi thi. Ek baar ASC mein Saban tumhare paas aaya aur poocha ki “Aamir bhai, tumko kisne maara?” Tumne Salman ka naam bataya. Iske baad un logon ne khud bola ki haan, bhai, isko marenge—apne dost ko hi.

Phir agle din, yani 20 August ko, jab hum sab Physics Lab mein the, Salman, Saban aur Samar apne group ke saath aaye aur Nishant ko washroom mein pakadne ke baad Physics Lab mein us par haath chhod diya aur usko maara. Hum logon ka group aadha bahar aur 2-3 log andar the. Jab tak hum kuch karte, woh log bahar bhaag gaye. Tab Gunjan Mam bhi aa gayi aur hum log class mein aa gaye.
Uske baad Computer wale Sir, jo Supriya Mam ke bhai hain, unhone tumhe, Nishant, Mayank aur Uday ko bulaya. Sir ne samjhaya ki ye sab mat karo, tum log senior ho aur tumhari baat koi nahi sunega, sab in logon ki baat maanenge. Humne Sir ko bhi poora matter short mein explain kiya. Sir ne Salman, Samar aur Saban ke naam likhe aur chale gaye.

Sir ke aane ka reason ye bhi tha ki last period mein jab Nishant paani bharne gaya tha, tab in logon ne usko pakad liya tha. Wahan humare class se Uday, Sudhanshu, Vanshdeep aur baaki log group mein gaye the. Mayank us time nahi ja paya. Bell hone ke baad Mayank bhi wahan gaya. Wahan dono groups ke bahut log jama ho gaye the. Us waqt Nishant aur Salman haath mila rahe the aur bol rahe the ki matter khatam ho gaya. Bahut bheed ho gayi thi, isliye sab wapas class mein aa gaye.

Lekin departure time mein jab hum bus ke liye ja rahe the, Salman ne Mayank ka haath pakadkar kheenchha aur Samar ko muh dikha kar bola ki “haan, yahi hai.”
Phir full confidence mein Mayank ko Indra Stadium mein maarne ke liye bulaya. Mayank ne us waqt josh mein “theek hai” bol diya. Uske baad Salman ne tumhe “Aamir” bolkar kaha ki “tere se matter solve ho gaya Aamir, ye Mayank ko batate hain,” aur Mayank ko dhamki bhi di. Uske baad sab log bus mein chale gaye.

Sir ko ye sab batate time ye bhi zaroor bolna ki ye koi nayi kahani nahi hai jo hum ab bana rahe hain. Independence Day wale incident ke baare mein Prince Sir ko pata hai. Supriya Mam ke bhai, Computer wale Sir ko bhi humne 20 August wale matter ke baare mein bataya tha aur unhone Salman, Samar aur Saban ke naam likhe the. Independence Day wale thappad aur chashma girne wali baat Kanak Mam ko bhi usi din Instagram par batayi gayi thi. Sir chahein to in sab teachers se confirm kar sakte hain.

Bas sir ko calmly aur honestly poora sequence batana. Kisi baat ko badha-chadha kar mat bolna, kyunki jo hua hai wahi hum sabko batana hai.
